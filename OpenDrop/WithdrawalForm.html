Legal

Right of Withdrawal

If you bought OpenDrop Pro as a consumer in the EU, the UK, or another jurisdiction with an equivalent statutory right, you have 14 days to withdraw from the contract without giving any reason. Submit the form below and we will respond within 1 business day.

14 Day window

You have 14 calendar days from the date of purchase to withdraw. The form below works for purchases made on Apple App Store, Google Play, and Stripe (desktop).

What we do

We acknowledge your request within 1 business day, instruct the original purchase platform to refund you (or send you the platform's refund-request link), and confirm when it is processed.

Refund timing

  • Apple App Store: 3 to 10 business days
  • Google Play: 1 to 14 business days
  • Stripe: 5 to 10 business days

Delete account too?

Withdrawing from your subscription does not delete your OpenDrop account. Use the hosted deletion page or in-app Settings to remove your account record and device entries:

opendrop.n-fantinodyer.workers.dev/delete-account

Just want to cancel?

If you only want to stop your subscription from renewing at the end of the current term, you do not need this form. Cancel from your purchase platform's Subscriptions screen. See Terms of Service Section 8.

Submit a withdrawal request

All fields with * are required. We treat your submission as an "unambiguous statement of withdrawal" under Directive 2011/83/EU Article 11(1).

Withdrawal request received

We have logged your withdrawal request. You will get an acknowledgment email at the address you provided within 1 business day. If you do not see it (check spam too), reply to that thread or email support@nfdgames.com.

Who this applies to

The right of withdrawal on this page is granted by:

  • Directive 2011/83/EU on consumer rights, Articles 9 to 16, as transposed into each EU member state's national law.
  • The Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 (United Kingdom), regulations 27 to 38.
  • Analogous statutory rights in other jurisdictions (for example, certain Swiss, Norwegian, and Icelandic consumer-protection regimes).

It applies if you are a consumer (a natural person acting for purposes outside your trade, business, craft, or profession) who purchased an OpenDrop Pro subscription from NFDGames LLC.

Important. Under Article 16(m) of the Consumer Rights Directive, the 14-day withdrawal right does not apply to "the supply of digital content which is not supplied on a tangible medium if the performance has begun with the consumer's prior express consent and his acknowledgment that he thereby loses his right of withdrawal." When you purchase OpenDrop Pro and tap "Confirm" or "Buy", you give that express consent and acknowledgment so Pro unlocks immediately. If you would rather preserve your withdrawal right, ask us to delay activation until day 15 by emailing support@nfdgames.com before you complete checkout.

We honor withdrawal requests received within 14 days regardless of the Article 16(m) waiver, as a matter of customer service, on a pro-rata refund basis (the price of the days you had access is retained, the rest is refunded). Where mandatory consumer law of your country grants you a stronger right, that stronger right applies.

What happens after you submit

  • You receive an automated confirmation that we got your form (from Formspree, our form processor).
  • NFDGames LLC acknowledges your withdrawal by email within 1 business day.
  • We instruct the platform that processed your purchase (Apple, Google, or Stripe) to issue a refund of all payments received from you. Where we cannot instruct the platform directly (Apple in particular controls App Store refunds), we email you the platform's refund-request link and follow up to verify it was processed.
  • The refund is issued using the same means of payment as your original transaction. You will not incur any fees for the refund.
  • If you exercised your withdrawal right before the 14-day period and we already began performance with your express consent, we may charge a pro-rata amount for the days you had Pro access, in line with Directive 2011/83/EU Article 14(3).

What this page does not affect

  • Auto-renewal cancellation. If you simply want to stop your subscription from auto-renewing at the end of the current term, you do not need to use this form. Open Settings on your purchase platform (Apple ID Settings → Subscriptions, Google Play → Subscriptions, or the Stripe customer portal) and cancel from there. See Section 8 of our Terms of Service.
  • Platform-policy refunds. Apple, Google, and Stripe have their own consumer-friendly refund processes that may extend beyond the 14-day window. Use them in parallel with this page.
  • Statutory warranty rights. Your mandatory consumer-warranty rights under EU and UK law (e.g., conformity rights under Directive (EU) 2019/770 on the supply of digital content) are independent of the withdrawal right and remain available after the 14-day period.
  • Account deletion. Withdrawing from your subscription does not delete your OpenDrop account. To delete your account, use the hosted deletion page at opendrop.n-fantinodyer.workers.dev/delete-account or follow Section 11 of our Privacy Policy.
