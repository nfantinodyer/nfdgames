Features

What makes OpenDrop different from cloud storage and AirDrop.

Instant Transfers

Direct local network transfers at maximum speed. No file size limits, no waiting for uploads.

Secure by Design

Encrypted connections via Cloudflare Tunnels with unique session keys. Your files never touch a third-party server.

Works Anywhere

Not just local Wi-Fi. Cloudflare tunneling lets you transfer files from anywhere, even over mobile data.

Cross-Platform

Windows and Linux servers with Android and iOS apps. Transfer between any device.

Zero Config

Launch the server and scan a QR code. That's it. No port forwarding, no accounts required.

Pro: Cloud Sync

Optional upgrade for auto-connect, remote folder access, and persistent connection URLs.

How it works

Up and running in under a minute.

1

Launch the Server

Download and run OpenDrop on your Windows or Linux PC. It starts automatically and creates a secure tunnel.

2

Scan the QR Code

Open the OpenDrop app on your phone and scan the QR code. Connected instantly.

3

Transfer Files

Browse and download files from your PC, or send files from your phone. Done.
