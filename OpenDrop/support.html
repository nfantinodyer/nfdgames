Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Why won't my phone connect?

Make sure both devices are on the same network, or use the Cloudflare tunnel URL. Try refreshing the QR code on the server.

Are my files secure?

Yes! OpenDrop uses end-to-end encryption with unique session keys. Your files are never stored on our servers.

What's the file size limit?

There's no file size limit for local transfers. Large files transfer at your network's maximum speed.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes! OpenDrop is available on both Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store). A macOS server version is currently in development.

How do I transfer photos from Android to PC wirelessly?

Open OpenDrop on your PC to create a secure tunnel. Scan the QR code with your Android phone. Select your photos and tap send. They will appear instantly in your PC's Downloads folder.

How is OpenDrop different from Snapdrop?

Unlike Snapdrop which requires WebRTC and internet for signaling, OpenDrop runs a native server on your device for faster, more reliable connections that work even without active internet.
