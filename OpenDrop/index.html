Security & Trust

End-to-end encrypted

ChaCha20-Poly1305 with HKDF-derived keys. Only your devices can decrypt your files.

HMAC-SHA256 signatures

Every request signed with your account's shared secret. No replay, no spoofing.

Relay sees ciphertext only

If your devices can reach each other directly — over your LAN or a peer-to-peer connection — they do. Otherwise our encrypted relay forwards bytes it can't decrypt.

No accounts on Free

Sign in only when you want Pro multi-device sync. Otherwise, totally anonymous.

How to Transfer Files with OpenDrop

Up and running in under a minute.

1

Launch the Server

Run OpenDrop on your PC. It starts a local server, broadcasts itself on your Wi-Fi via mDNS, and connects to OpenDrop's own encrypted relay. All automatic, with nothing extra to download.

2

Scan the QR Code

Open the OpenDrop app on your phone and scan the QR code on your PC. Your phone connects using the fastest path available, either local Wi-Fi or the encrypted relay.

3

Transfer Files

Browse your PC's files from your phone, send photos and documents back, or share entire folders. Files transfer directly between your devices.
