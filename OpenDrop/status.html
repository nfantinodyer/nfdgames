About this page

Service statuses are refreshed every 15 minutes by a Cloudflare Worker cron that probes each external dependency and writes the result to KV. The page reads from that KV snapshot via the public API at opendrop.n-fantinodyer.workers.dev/api/status.

If the snapshot is older than 45 minutes, or the API itself is unreachable, the page falls back to manually-maintained rows and shows a notice at the top. Cloudflare and Worker outages will silence the cron, so during edge-wide incidents the page may stop updating until the platform recovers, that is itself a useful signal.

If you are seeing a problem that this page does not reflect, email support@nfdgames.com with the subject line "Status Report" and a one-line description. The fastest path to a fix is a clear repro and the timestamp you first saw the issue (in UTC if possible). Local-LAN and BLE transfers between your own devices keep working regardless of what shows here.

Status definitions:

  • Operational   All known functionality of the service is available.
  • Degraded   The service is partially impaired: elevated latency, intermittent errors, or a feature subset down.
  • Major outage   The service is unreachable or returning errors for most requests.
  • Maintenance   Planned maintenance window in progress; impact described in the incident notes.

What we cannot guarantee

OpenDrop's cloud services run on third-party infrastructure (Cloudflare, Fly.io, Oracle Cloud, Firebase, Apple, Google Play, Microsoft Store, Stripe, RevenueCat). When one of those providers has an outage, our services that depend on them go down with them and we report that here under the affected row. Per Section 11 and Section 18 of our Terms of Service, third-party outages are outside our reasonable control and do not constitute a breach of the Terms by NFDGames LLC.

For most users, the best mitigation is to keep at least one direct-LAN pairing between two of your devices. LAN and Bluetooth transfers don't touch our cloud and continue working when the cloud doesn't.
